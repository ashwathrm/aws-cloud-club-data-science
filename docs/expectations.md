# Group Expectations

## Meetings
- We will meet weekly at ___
- Members should attend meetings and communicate scheduling conflicts in advance.

## Time Commitment
- Each member is expected to contribute at least 2-3 hours per week.
- Members should complete assigned tasks by agreed deadlines and provide regular progress updates.

## Communication
- Use the group chat for questions, updates, and collaboration.
- Communicate any blockers or difficulties as soon as possible.

## Falling Behind
- If a member is falling behind, they should notify the group chat within 48 hours.
- The team will discuss blockers and redistribute tasks at the next meeting if necessary.
- Prioritize completing core project features before optional additions.
